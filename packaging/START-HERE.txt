SOUNDFORM V2.0 FOR WINDOWS

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Double-click SoundForm-V2.0-Setup-x64.exe and follow the prompts.
3. Open SoundForm V2.0 from the Start menu. The installer also offers a desktop shortcut.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Setup installs for the current user and
includes Python and the app dependencies. Uninstall from Windows Settings.
The app and installer are unsigned.

Audio analysis, site import, meshing and exports run locally. Experimental AI
pavilion generation requires your own ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and internet access.

If startup fails, send the author the file:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Soundform\startup-error.log
